# College ERP Roles & Permissions Matrix

## System Roles
1. `SUPER_ADMIN`: Full system-wide administrative control.
2. `COLLEGE_ADMIN`: College-level administrative and management operations.
3. `PRINCIPAL`: High-level oversight, executive dashboards, analytics, announcements.
4. `VICE_PRINCIPAL`: Operations oversight, academics, attendance, examinations.
5. `HOD`: Departmental management, faculty workload, student tracking, approvals.
6. `FACULTY`: Subject management, student attendance marking, assignment creation, grading.
7. `STUDENT`: Self-service portal for attendance, timetable, results, fees, study material, placements.
8. `PARENT`: Read-only view for linked student performance, fees, attendance, notices.
9. `ACCOUNTANT`: Fee structure setup, payment collection, receipts, financial reports.
10. `LIBRARIAN`: Book inventory, cataloging, issue/return processing, fine tracking.
11. `EXAM_CELL`: Exam scheduling, marks entry, SGPA/CGPA calculation, result publication.
12. `PLACEMENT_OFFICER`: Drive creation, eligibility checks, student shortlist management.
13. `NON_TEACHING_STAFF`: Permission-scoped support role.
